# Playground figures: what each one answers

Generated by `analysis_pipe/playground_figures.py` into `results/figures/playground/`
(PNG for slides, PDF for manuscripts). Tidy data behind them:
`results/data/playground_tidy_behavioral.csv`. Rerun the script after new
sessions land; pending cells are drawn as "pending" and fill in automatically.

The R/Quarto pipeline (`analysis.qmd`) still covers the April benchmark CSVs
(`results/model.results/`); this script covers the FarmShare smoke-ladder era
(30-trial generation runs, AB label fix, PriDe, embeddings).

## Fig 1 — validity gates (`fig1_validity_gates`)
Which model x prompt x label x stimulus cells produce interpretable choices at
all. Tracking = the model picks the same feature when image order is swapped.
Cells below the 0.70 gate, or position-locked, tell us nothing about shape
bias: the measure fails before the construct is reached. This is the
methods-fragility result and the reading guide for every other figure.

## Fig 2 — shape bias where valid (`fig2_shape_bias`)
The headline result: P(shape match) with Wilson CIs for gate-passing cells,
against chance and the adult human rate from our online sample. Answers
whether models that can do the task prefer shape, and whether the preference
depends on prompt condition (similarity / category / noun) or stimulus set.

## Fig 3 — naming effect (`fig3_naming_effect`)
The Landau-Smith-Jones question: does a count noun ("this is a dax") raise
shape choice relative to a similarity-only prompt? Paired dots per model;
faded when either cell fails the gate.

## Fig 4 — answer-format sensitivity (`fig4_label_format`)
Same task, 1/2 vs A/B response labels. Points off the diagonal mean the
measured "shape bias" partly reflects response-format handling rather than
visual preference. Relevant to the argument that instrument choices carry
psychological assumptions.

## Fig 5 — AB label-fix effect (`fig5_ab_label_fix`)
Methods figure for the July-28 bug fix: archived runs labeled images
"Image 1/2" while asking for answers "A/B". Arrows show tracking and shape
rate moving from the mismatched run to the fixed rerun. Documents why the
pre-fix AB data were archived.

## Fig 6 — position-bias correction (`fig6_position_bias_correction`)
Latent (logit) shape preference after swap averaging, full permutation, and
PriDe debiasing, next to raw generation. Asks whether a shape preference
exists underneath the position bias that generation shows. Picks up the
2026-07-30 PriDe CSVs automatically when the finalize job writes them; until
then it shows the numeric-label rows from the July runs.

## Fig 7 — vision tower vs behavior (`fig7_vision_vs_behavior`)
Panel A: shape rate in vision-tower embeddings (centered cosine) for novel
stimuli and Geirhos cue-conflict images. Panel B: embedding shape rate vs
behavioral shape choice. Dissociation here supports the claim that shape
information present in vision is not used by the language side.

## Fig 7b — benchmark vs Smith embeddings (`fig7b_benchmark_vs_smith`)
Same vision-tower readout for the full ladder (including qwen3.5-9b / 27b),
comparing novel-object benchmark stimuli to the Linda Smith probe set.
Panel A: paired shape rates with bootstrap CIs. Panel B: embedding vs
behavioral similarity (numeric) for each stimulus set (filled = benchmark,
open = Smith). Needs `session_2026-07-31_farmshare/embedding_robust_fill_large.json`
and `embedding_smith_probe.json` from `scripts/run_embedding_bench_smith_31.sbatch`;
until those land, missing cells are marked pending.

## Fig 8 — word generality (`fig8_word_generality`)
Shape choice for each of the five pseudo-words. If the noun effect is about
labeling rather than a specific string, lines should be flat.
